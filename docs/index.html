Overview

FK Cart Recovery Pro is a complete multi-channel WooCommerce cart abandonment recovery solution. It captures abandoned carts in real-time as customers type their information on checkout, and automatically triggers personalized recovery sequences across Email, WhatsApp, SMS, and Telegram.

Multi-Channel Recovery

Dispatch automated recovery messages via Email, WhatsApp (UltraMsg, Twilio, WhatsApp Cloud API), SMS (Twilio, Vonage), and Telegram.

Dynamic Coupons

Generate individual, single-use, auto-expiring WooCommerce discount coupons tied directly to recovery campaigns.

1-Click Restoration

Direct customers back to checkout with all products, quantities, and coupons pre-loaded securely via encrypted tokens.

In-Depth Analytics

Track recovery rates, total revenue recovered, open rates, click-through rates, and export CSV reports effortlessly.

Installation

You can install the plugin either via the WordPress admin dashboard or via FTP / SFTP.

Method 1: WordPress Admin Upload (Recommended)

  1. Log in to your WordPress Admin dashboard.
  2. Navigate to Plugins → Add New.
  3. Click on the Upload Plugin button at the top of the page.
  4. Choose the fk-cart-recovery.zip archive and click Install Now.
  5. Once installed, click Activate Plugin.

Method 2: Manual Upload via FTP

  1. Extract the contents of fk-cart-recovery.zip on your local computer.
  2. Connect to your server using an FTP client (FileZilla, Cyberduck, etc.).
  3. Upload the unzipped fk-cart-recovery directory to /wp-content/plugins/.
  4. Go to Plugins → Installed Plugins in WordPress admin and click Activate.

Quick Start Guide

  1. After activation, a new menu item labeled Cart Recovery will appear in your admin sidebar.
  2. Navigate to Cart Recovery → Settings.
  3. Set the Cart Abandonment Cut-off Time (default is 60 minutes). If a customer leaves the checkout page without completing their purchase, their cart will be classified as abandoned after this interval.
  4. Switch to the Campaigns tab and review the pre-configured recovery sequence or create new ones.
  5. Test a sample recovery email or notification to verify deliverability.

Email Channel Setup

Email is the foundational recovery channel. FK Cart Recovery Pro includes a responsive email layout with custom branding, logo support, item tables, and open/click tracking.

Key Settings:

  • From Name: The sender name displayed in customer inboxes (e.g., Your Store Name).
  • From Email: The verified sender address from your domain.
  • Reply-To: Email address where customer responses will be sent.
  • Email Logo: Upload your store logo to display at the header of recovery emails.
  • Open & Click Tracking: Automatically inserts an invisible tracking pixel and wraps recovery links to measure engagement.
Deliverability Tip

For optimal inbox placement, use a transactional SMTP service (e.g. SendGrid, Mailgun, Postmark, or Amazon SES) with proper SPF, DKIM, and DMARC DNS records configured on your domain.

WhatsApp Channel Setup

Reach customers directly on WhatsApp for open rates exceeding 90%. The plugin supports three industry-standard providers:

1. UltraMsg

UltraMsg provides simple QR-code based WhatsApp gateway integration.

  • Create an account at UltraMsg.com and generate an Instance.
  • Enter your Instance ID and Token in Cart Recovery → Settings → WhatsApp.

2. Twilio WhatsApp

Enterprise-grade WhatsApp messaging via the Twilio API.

  • Obtain your Account SID and Auth Token from the Twilio Console.
  • Enter your approved Twilio WhatsApp Sender number (e.g. whatsapp:+14155238886).

3. WhatsApp Cloud API (Meta Official)

Direct integration with Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform.

  • Create an app in the Meta for Developers portal.
  • Enter your Phone Number ID and Permanent Access Token.

SMS Channel Setup

Send concise SMS reminders with direct 1-click cart restoration links. The plugin integrates natively with:

1. Twilio SMS

  • Account SID: Your Twilio account identifier.
  • Auth Token: Your Twilio API authentication token.
  • From Phone Number: Your purchased Twilio phone number or Alphanumeric Sender ID.

2. Vonage (formerly Nexmo)

  • API Key: Your Vonage dashboard API key.
  • API Secret: Your Vonage dashboard secret token.
  • From Name / Number: Approved sender ID.

Telegram Channel Setup

Recover carts via Telegram Bot messaging with verified account linking.

Step-by-Step Telegram Setup:

  1. Open Telegram and search for @BotFather.
  2. Send /newbot and follow the instructions to name your bot.
  3. Copy the provided Bot Token and paste it into Cart Recovery → Settings → Telegram.
  4. Click Register Webhook to automatically configure the Telegram callback URL.
Secure Verification

To ensure customer privacy, when a customer starts the bot, a verification link is sent to their registered email before their Telegram account is linked.

Campaigns & Automation Engine

Build staged recovery sequences tailored to customer behavior.

Sequence Delay Channel Objective

Step 2: Urgency / Social Proof 24 hours Email Highlight item popularity, reviews, and stock scarcity.

Rule Conditions

You can filter which carts trigger specific campaigns using rule conditions:

  • Cart Total: Only trigger if cart subtotal is greater than, less than, or equal to a target amount.
  • Item Count: Target shoppers with multiple items in their basket.
  • User Role: Differentiate campaigns between Guests and Registered Customers.

Dynamic Discount Coupons

FK Cart Recovery Pro allows you to automatically generate unique, single-use WooCommerce discount coupons for each recovery email or message.

Configuration Options:

  • Discount Type: Percentage discount (e.g., 10%) or Fixed Cart discount (e.g., $15).
  • Coupon Expiry: Set how many hours or days the coupon remains valid before expiring automatically.
  • Single Use Restriction: Coupons are automatically restricted for single-use to prevent sharing on coupon aggregator websites.
  • Auto-Application: When the customer clicks the recovery link, the coupon is applied to their checkout automatically without manual entry.

1-Click Recovery Links

Every recovery message contains a personalized recovery link generated using a cryptographically secure token.

  • Session Restoration: Reconstructs the customer's exact cart items and quantities.
  • Auto Login (Registered Users): For returning customers, optionally authenticates their session securely.
  • Form Pre-filling: Pre-populates checkout fields with the captured customer name, email, phone, and address.

Reports & Analytics

Monitor your recovery funnel directly from the Cart Recovery → Analytics dashboard.

  • Recoverable Revenue: Total value of currently abandoned carts.
  • Recovered Revenue: Total monetary value of completed orders generated through recovery links.
  • Recovery Rate: Percentage of abandoned sessions that converted into completed orders.
  • Channel Breakdown: Compare conversion rates across Email, WhatsApp, SMS, and Telegram.
  • CSV Export: Download comprehensive reports for accounting or third-party CRM integration.

GDPR & Privacy Compliance

Protect customer privacy and maintain full compliance with international privacy regulations:

  • Checkout Consent Checkbox: Add an optional or mandatory consent checkbox at checkout before capturing guest data.
  • Automatic Data Pruning: Automatically delete recovered and abandoned cart records after a configurable number of days (e.g. 30, 60, or 90 days).
  • WordPress Privacy Tools Integration: Fully integrated with WordPress core Personal Data Export and Personal Data Erasure tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this plugin require WooCommerce?

Yes. FK Cart Recovery Pro is specifically designed for WooCommerce and requires WooCommerce 5.0 or higher.

How does cart tracking work?

When customers enter their email or phone number on the checkout page, the plugin captures the details asynchronously via AJAX. If the order is not placed within the cut-off time (e.g., 60 minutes), the cart is flagged as abandoned.

Are external messaging services required?

For Email recovery, the plugin uses standard WordPress / SMTP mailing and requires no paid external service. For WhatsApp or SMS, you will need an account with one of the supported providers (UltraMsg, Twilio, Vonage, or Meta).

Is the plugin compatible with High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS)?

Yes. FK Cart Recovery Pro is fully compatible with WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS) and custom cart tables.
